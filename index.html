01 / Profile

Preparedness is
a shared responsibility.

My work brings together disaster risk management, humanitarian coordination and practical planning. Over more than two decades, I have worked across UNICEF, Oxfam, the International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies, and Nigeria’s National Emergency Management Agency.

I support teams and institutions to understand risks, clarify responsibilities and prepare for floods, disease outbreaks, conflict and displacement. My approach combines government partnership, field experience and data-informed decisions, with a focus on children and vulnerable communities.
